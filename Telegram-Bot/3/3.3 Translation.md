## Getting Acquainted with the Capabilities

In the previous section, we learned how to create a Telegram bot. Now let's look at what functions BotFather offers and how they can be useful for your future work.

Open BotFather. Go to "Menu" and select "Edit your bots". In the message that appears, choose your bot, and a menu with advanced settings will open:

**"API Token"** – You can view your bot's token or replace it in case of theft by clicking "Revoke your token".

**"Payments"** – Used to connect payment systems within the bot.

**"Delete Bot"** – Deletes your bot and its token accordingly.

**"Edit Bot"** – This section consists of 6 items, which we will look at next:

1. **"Edit Name"** – You can change the bot's name.
2. **"Edit About"** – You can change the bot's description (short info) located in its profile.
3. **"Edit Description"** – You can change the text that users will see before they press the "Start" button in the bot.
4. **"Edit Description Picture"** – You can change the image shown to users before they press the "Start" button in the bot.
5. **"Edit Botpic"** – You can change the bot's profile picture (avatar).
6. **"Edit Commands"** – You can add quick-access commands that will appear in the "Menu".

## New Feature

In one of the recent Telegram updates, bot owners can now edit their bot's profile directly from the bot itself. The editing functionality is significantly less than in BotFather, but from the bot you can:

1. Select / change the bot's avatar.
2. Change the bot's name.
3. Add / change the bot's description (short info) located in its profile.
4. Add / change the text that users will see before they press the "Start" button in the bot.
5. Add / change quick commands that will be available in the menu.
6. Click the "Change bot settings" button to go to BotFather for more fine-tuning.
